About us

Melken Handyman Solutions is a veteran-owned company founded by Kendall Felder, a Navy veteran and experienced project manager. The company combines over 30 years of technical and operational expertise with a mission-first mindset to deliver trustworthy, high-quality handyman services. Serving homeowners, landlords, and small business owners, the team prioritizes punctuality, clear communication, and dependable results using modern tools and streamlined scheduling technology to make home repair easy and efficient.

Frequently Asked Questions

🔧 General Questions

What kind of services do you offer?

We offer a wide range of general handyman services including drywall repair, pressure cleaning, gutter cleaning, light electrical and plumbing, appliance installation, smart home setup, rental turnovers, safety upgrades for aging-in-place, and more. We do not perform any licensed, structural, or hazardous work.

Are you licensed and insured?

In Florida, general handyman work under $2,500 does not require a license. We are fully insured and compliant with Florida regulations. Any work requiring a licensed contractor will be referred appropriately.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes! We offer a free in-person assessment to evaluate your needs and provide a written estimate before work begins.

💳 Pricing & Payment

How is your pricing structured?

We price by the job or by the hour depending on the service. Our standard handyman rate is $85/hour (2-hour minimum). Larger jobs are quoted individually.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes, we require a 50% deposit to secure your service appointment. The balance is due upon completion.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept cash, credit/debit cards, Zelle, Venmo, Apple Pay, and business checks (with prior approval). Clients using peer-to-peer apps assume all risks associated with those platforms.

What if I cancel my appointment?

Cancellations made less than 24 hours before your appointment will incur a $50 fee. Please give us as much notice as possible if your plans change.

🏠 Service Details

Do you work on weekends or evenings?

Appointments are generally scheduled Monday–Friday. Evening and weekend appointments may be available by request for an additional fee.

Do I need to be home during the service?

For initial walk-throughs, yes. For follow-up work, you may choose to be present or provide access. Clear instructions and safe entry must be arranged.

Do you guarantee your work?

Yes! We offer a 30-day workmanship guarantee. This excludes damage caused by misuse, third-party interference, or faulty customer-supplied materials.

🧑🏽‍🤝‍🧑🏼 Customer Experience

How do you ensure safety and respect in my home?

Our team is trained to treat every client and space with respect, courtesy, and professionalism. We do not tolerate discrimination or harassment of any kind, and we expect the same from all customers.

Do you take photos of your work?

We may take photos for documentation or marketing, but we will never use identifying photos without your written consent.

🧓 Specialty Services

Do you offer aging-in-place or safety upgrades?

Yes! We offer grab bar installation, step modifications, lighting improvements, and other safety enhancements to support clients who want to age comfortably and safely in their homes.

Can you help with rental property turnovers?

Absolutely. We offer basic and comprehensive turnover services for property managers and landlords, including cleaning, minor repairs, fixture updates, and safety checks.
